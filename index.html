Local Storage & Privacy Information

How Paisa Plan securely protects your financial data

🔒
100% Client-Side Privacy:

Paisa Plan uses the browser's window.localStorage engine. Your expense data is saved exclusively inside your current web browser and never leaves your computer.

No backend servers, no logins, no cloud tracking, and no paid third-party APIs are required. Please note that data stored in LocalStorage does not automatically synchronize across different browsers or devices.
